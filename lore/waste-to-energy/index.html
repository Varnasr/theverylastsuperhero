Waste-to-Energy Systems

The pilot plants Rohan Kapoor reviews — revivals of the old Belgian system that worked at Gazipur in the 2020s, promising to revolutionise Delhi's energy grid if the emissions controls hold.

Consultations around going back to the old Belgian waste-to-energy system that worked in Gazipur in the 2020s. New pilot models, reviewed for emissions integrity.

The plants have a name on them. Waste to Energy by Thakur Green Developers — on a street sign Rohan passes leaving the station, where it stirs a memory he cannot quite reach.

The new WTE systems could revolutionise Delhi’s energy grid if the emissions controls work as promised.

If. The whole book turns on that word. A cleaner, more efficient pilot, a model for the whole zone — and a fourteen-year-old who has spent her entire life hearing promises like it.

This is the part of the novel that is not speculative. The battle over Delhi’s waste-to-energy plants is real and ongoing; the book is dedicated in part to someone fighting it. See the campaign.

The archive holds no human account of this — only the transmission. Record one.
