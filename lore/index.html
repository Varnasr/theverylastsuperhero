Technology · Archive Memory System | "Story Salt"

Salt Lamps

Crystalline lamps of Khewra salt blended with hysterons, carved into precision lattices that record, reflect and relay. Not Himalayan — that part was always a marketing scam.

Symbol · Resistance Iconography | One Feather Missing

The Firebird

A firebird with wings mid-rise and one feather always missing. Not on uniforms — there were none. Not on records — they were erased. Everywhere else.

Technology · WTE | Emissions Integrity

Waste-to-Energy Systems

The pilot plants Rohan Kapoor reviews — revivals of the old Belgian system that worked at Gazipur in the 2020s, promising to revolutionise Delhi's energy grid if the emissions controls hold.

◉ Salt Lamp testimony held

Technology · Shape Memory Materials | Salt Lamp Component

Hysterons

Memory materials — Nitinol and other Shape Memory Materials — blended into the salt. Combined with halite under the right compression and lattice integrity, the result can remember.

System · Predictive Autonomous Interface

P.A.I.

Officially the Predictive Autonomous Interface. In practice, Pandemonium Algorithmic Indoctrination — it monitored for deviance, and it did not correct. It punished.

System · Public Access Intelligence

p.ai

The same system after an eleven-year-old broke it and spent five more years teaching it to forget the words "command" and "obedience". The lowercase is the whole point.

Character · Protagonist

Sumati

The girl who finds her father's Salt Lamp and reawakens the forgotten archive.

Character · Climate Scientist | Whistleblower

Rohan Kapoor

Twenty years of climate adaptation research, work on the Yamuna Action Plan, and a cooling suit he still finds fascinating. His disappearance sets Sumati's journey in motion.

System · State Curriculum | Science Under Siege

Indigenous Knowledge Systems

The curriculum public universities were pushed to adopt — a blend of astrology, myth and nationalist revisionism. Questioning it made you anti-national.

◉ Salt Lamp testimony held

Character · Crow Guardian

Chandra

A large crow that does not scatter. An ancient manuscript in the Treasure Room holds that ancestors return to us as ants or crows, to guide us through life — as the moon, Chandra, guides the stars.

Character · Sumati's Mother

Meera

Present most often as a lagging holographic call, and terrifying even in that form. With Rohan, she fought to expose what was being hidden — before she had to go away.

Character · Elder Leader | Nandini's

Nandini Maa

Mrs Nandini Sharma, who taught at the collapsed Sarvodaya Vidyalaya and now runs the tea stall at the end of the platform. Finds a girl who has missed her train and says: we'll figure it out together.

Character · Streetwise Ally

Aasma

A clever, fast-talking girl living on the fringes of the surveillance grid, who knows which corners the cameras have never learned.

Character · Tech Sage of the Early Resistance

Myaknju

The tech sage who designed the Salt Lamps — combining the old wisdom of stones that remember with acoustic resonance and nanoscale tuning.

Infrastructure · Sensory Overlays

Virtual Layer Flooring

Delicate patterned surfaces laid in schools and archives for story-triggered audio playback.

Object · Data Storage

Memory Tablets

Portable bricks used to capture oral testimony. Activated by Salt Lamps or object tags.

Character · Rohan's Colleague

Mr Joshi

The colleague Rohan steps off the train to hand documents to — for two minutes, at a station with only two platforms.

Character · Archive Keeper

Prahlad

A gaunt, soft-spoken man who keeps what is left of the archive, and knows the difference between a record and a rumour.

Character · Climate Solutions

Dr Gupta

The colleague Rohan is travelling to meet, to discuss climate change solutions for the city.

Object · Vikram Protocol

Obj-V078

Classified object tag leading to corrupted fragments of betrayal and exile.

Creature · Companion | The Dog Who Listens

Buddy

The stray who settles beside Sumati on a station bench and stays. A dog who listens harder than most people.

Techwear · Climate Adaptation

Cooling Suits

Not an accessory. Extended exposure without one means heat stroke, dehydration, and death — and most suits are third-hand, patchy, humming faintly.

Creature · Mongoose

Moti

Watchful and nosy, known to carry messages or disrupt AI drones when trained.

Creature · Ginger Cat | Nandini's

Momo

Nandini's fat ginger cat, and a plump distraction who upends more than one scan sweep.

Creature · Parrot | Nandini's

Rani

Nandini's talking parrot, perched on a wooden stand, with opinions about the weather.

Network · Underground Memory Movement

The Resistance

Formed by librarians, scientists, elders and teens — built on preserving truth.

Infrastructure · Hyperloop | Maglev

The Hyper Express

Delhi to Kolkata in six hours, on journey times that showed as impossible a decade ago. Trains become places to think things out.

Location · Archive Vault | In the Library

The Treasure Room

A small room off the library, crammed with oddities, where Kishore Mehta gathers explanations to mysterious phenomena — and where all Salt Lamps reconnect.

Location · Station | Two Platforms

Serenity Junction

A quaint platform in Kishanganj with memorabilia on the walls, where the train stops for two minutes and a father steps off.

Event · Environmental & Political Catastrophe

The Collapse

The environmental devastation that led to the downfall of cities and the rise of the Resistance.

Location · Inadaptable Zone (Category 2)

Kishanganj

The town where Serenity Junction sits. Once lush-green fields, now a Category 2 inadaptable zone — 55.6°C average surface temperature and a population down 43% since 2060.

Event · Two Decades | Ended 2060

The Age of Heroes

For twenty years heroes soared in plasma suits and gravity-defying capes, and India worshipped them. The 2060 Disclosures ended it.

Event · c. 2035–2055 | Twenty Years

The Golden Age of Heroes

Twenty years when plasma-suited vigilantes, genetically enhanced defenders and state-funded climate responders dominated global headlines. It ended in a tribunal.

Character · Fallen Hero | Weather Control

Captain Indra

He single-handedly evacuated thousands during the 2055 Bangladesh flooding. He also signed contracts with the corporations he claimed to fight, and so did twelve others.

Network · Hero Organisation | Defunct

Delhi Climate Brigade

Once a gleaming symbol of hope with headquarters in the heart of the capital. Its micro-climate shields masked rising temperatures rather than addressing their causes.

Network · Hero Organisation | Defunct

Quantum Corps

Masters of particle manipulation who could purify water with a touch and cleanse the air with a gesture. Their cyclone diversions worsened drought in vulnerable regions.

Character · VIKRAM-09 | The Name, and the Man Who Took It

Vikram Malhotra

Two people. Vikram was the last state-designed environmental hero, obedient to the end. Sanil took his name — not to honour him, but to mark the risk of forgetting.

Character · Librarian | also Rishi, also Axl

Kishore Mehta

A librarian of more than thirty years who keeps the Treasure Room and gathers explanations for mysterious phenomena. Also the eleven-year-old who broke P.A.I.

Character · Prototype | Decommissioned

Asha

An early state-designed hero who rerouted monsoon floods without state permission. She was decommissioned for it. Her name means hope, which the archive notes without comment.

Character · Wind-Manipulating Hero | Shrine Object

Vayu

The hero who promised clean air. Sumati's mother kept a shrine to him. That detail says more about the era than any tribunal record.
