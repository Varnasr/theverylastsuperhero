Privacy Policy

This site runs no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking cookies. What follows is the complete list of everything that does happen.

What is stored in your browser

If you switch between dark and light reading modes, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage under phw-theme. It never leaves your device and is never sent to us. Clearing your site data removes it.

What third parties see

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives your IP address and browser user-agent as part of serving the page, and may retain those in server logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google also receives your IP address when a page loads. No cookie is set by this request. If you would rather not make it, a content blocker will prevent it and the site falls back to your system fonts.

There are no other third-party requests. No embedded video, no social widgets, no pixels.

If you contact us or submit to the Memory Wall

If you email us, or send artwork or testimony through the Memory Wall, we keep your message and the name you gave so we can reply and, if you have agreed to it, display your contribution with attribution. We do not sell or share it, and we do not add you to any mailing list.

You can ask us to remove your contribution or your details at any time by writing to info@storywell.in.

Children

The novel is written for readers of around twelve and up. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and the Memory Wall asks for no more than a name and an email address.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. The full history of every version is public in the site's repository.
