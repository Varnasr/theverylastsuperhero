Illustrations

34 pieces made while writing the book. 14 of them made it in; the other 20 did not — rooms that were described and then cut, characters who lost their scene, versions that were nearly right. They are here because the world is bigger than the book that came out of it.

Artwork by Varna Sri Raman, generated with AI image tools from written prompts and then selected, refined and sequenced by hand. Free to share under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.
