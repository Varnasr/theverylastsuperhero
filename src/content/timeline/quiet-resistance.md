There is no founding. That is the point.

The Resistance did not fight with fire. It fought with documentation, doubt,
salt lamps, cracked badges and memory tablets that outlived the power grids —
and what resumes here resumes in the same register. A girl. A dog. A crow. A
librarian who has been doing it for thirty years and did not stop.

> Collapse doesn't mean the end. It means the truth has outlived its
> suppressors.

The [Firebird](/lore/the-firebird) is on walls again, drawn in charcoal, one
feather short. Nobody has announced anything.
