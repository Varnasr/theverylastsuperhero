An undercover journalist, a girl with no Obedience Index, and a field operative.

[Thakur GreenWorks](/lore/thakur-greenworks) was publicly clean and privately
toxic — fly ash every night between midnight and four, and disappearances that
never reached a file. The infiltration took its internal documents out to
servers beyond its reach.

Compare 2060. The first Disclosures came from a leak inside a system of
celebrities and were broadcast worldwide within days. This one is three people,
a facility perimeter, and a transmission. It is the same act performed without
any of the apparatus, which is the argument the whole book is making.
