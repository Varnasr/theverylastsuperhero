This is the event that made the case for heroes, and it is worth being precise
about it: the evacuation was real, the thousands were real, and the man who did
it was photographed doing it.

Mass climate migration intensified from here. Coastal evacuation policy produced
relief zones that were heavily militarised within a year, on the reasoning that
a desperate population is a security matter before it is a humanitarian one.

Five years later the tribunal would establish what else was true at the same
time. Both things stay in the record. The archive does not resolve them, because
the novel does not: the flood is why people believed, and the belief is what the
contracts were bought with.
