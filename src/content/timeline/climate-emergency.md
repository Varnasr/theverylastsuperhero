Before the Golden Age there is a decision, and the decision is structural rather
than heroic: faced with a crisis that needed institutions, the state funded
individuals instead.

The [Quantum Corps](/lore/quantum-corps) took planetary interventions —
redirecting hurricanes, manipulating rainfall, geoengineering at scale. The
[Delhi Climate Brigade](/lore/delhi-climate-brigade) took the local end: heat
shields, wildfire barriers, evacuations.

Both were idolised. Neither was audited.
