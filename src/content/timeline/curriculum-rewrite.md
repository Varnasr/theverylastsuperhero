Science was already under siege. Public universities had been pushed onto a
curriculum of astrology, myth and nationalist revisionism, filed under
[Indigenous Knowledge Systems](/lore/indigenous-knowledge-systems), and teacher
training had become a delivery mechanism for it.

What happens in 2061 is narrower and more surgical: the five years that had just
been exposed are rewritten, and the teachers who kept their own materials are
told to hand them in.

> Teaching from superseded material is a compliance matter.

The Disclosures had proved the heroes lied. This is the state deciding that the
proof itself is the problem, and that the fix is upstream of any argument — in
what a fourteen-year-old is taught happened.
