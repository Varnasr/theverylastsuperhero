A crowdsourced visual archive of the posters, placards and photography of India's protest movements, curated by Dr Manmohan Singh Fellows. Faces are taped over before anything is published, so the archive shows the signage and keeps the people holding it anonymous. Varna Sri Raman runs it, and you can add photographs to it. The Hindu interviewed Pallavi Nayak and her about it under the headline
[When protest becomes memory](https://www.thehindu.com/entertainment/art/jantar-mantar-genz-protest-neet-paper-leak-visual-archival-documentation-platform-democracy-by-design/article71300118.ece).
