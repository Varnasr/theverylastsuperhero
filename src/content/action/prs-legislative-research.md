Tracks every bill in Parliament, in plain English, with the text, the debate and the voting record. If you have ever wanted to know what was actually passed rather than what was announced, this is where you look. The novel's Curriculum Division rewrites five years of history in a single notice; PRS exists so that the real version stays legible while it is still happening.
