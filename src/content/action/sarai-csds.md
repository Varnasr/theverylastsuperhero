The research programme at the Centre for the Study of Developing Societies in Delhi, running since 2000 on media, information and the contemporary. It works by distributed fellowships and by building an archive — field notes, images, films, audio, periodicals — rather than by producing conclusions. The archivists in this book work the way Sarai works.
