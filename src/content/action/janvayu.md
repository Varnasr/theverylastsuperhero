Community air-quality monitoring in India — sensors, readings, and the work of turning a number into something a neighbourhood can act on. Kishanganj's environmental snapshot lists PM2.5 at 312 as a line item. JanVayu is people measuring the real thing, in the real places, and refusing to let it stay abstract.
