A free library in Bansa village, Hardoi district, Uttar Pradesh, started in 2020 and open to anyone — no bar on age, gender, caste or class. It grew from fifty books to five thousand and now reaches across fifty-four villages. Mr Mehta's library in this novel is not a metaphor. It is this, carried forward.
