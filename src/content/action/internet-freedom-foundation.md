Litigates and campaigns on surveillance, facial recognition, and the laws that govern them in India. They file the RTIs and the petitions that P.A.I. would have made impossible. Much of what the book imagines as settled — biometric gates, compliance scoring, opaque algorithms deciding access — is being argued over right now, and IFF is doing the arguing.
