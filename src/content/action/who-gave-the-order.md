A public record of police use of force in India, and of who authorised it. It holds a hard line that most reporting does not: an office with the power to order something is not an office shown to have ordered it. Documents are checked against originals, and official silence is filed too, with the date the answer was due. Another of the author's projects, and anyone can add a document.
