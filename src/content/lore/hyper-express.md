The air buzzes with the electric hum of the maglev tracks below, and beneath the
technological thrum lie more familiar sounds: chaiwallahs calling their wares,
children laughing, travellers greeting relatives in a dozen dialects. Station
robots announce gentle beeps in five languages, their soft blue guidance lights
creating paths through the crowd.

*Delhi to Kolkata in six hours.* Journey times that showed as impossible a decade
ago.

It stops at Serenity Junction for two minutes.
