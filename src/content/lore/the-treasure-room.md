Broken fans, old parchment, coins with holes, logs of rotting wood, shiny metal
balls of every size, lamps and lampshades, embroidered fabric, and bottles of
every colour.

It looks like hoarding. It is a filing system.

> The world is full of inexplicable things. The Treasure Room is where I gather
> explanations to many mysterious phenomena.

Somewhere in the piles is a tattered manuscript in an unfamiliar script that
explains why a crow has been following you. One of the last safe places where all
Salt Lamps reconnect.

Raided in 2072. Never entirely lost.
