Known both as Vikram and as Sanil.

*Another alleged sighting of Vikram Malhotra in the Yamuna flood plains*, the
anchor reports, her artificial smile never reaching her eyes. *The
self-proclaimed environmental hero claims to have developed a method to reverse
desertification with…*

Most people swipe the broadcast away. Fifteen years after the Disclosures,
superhero sightings are more punchline than news.

The archive is less relaxed about him. Obj-V078 is tagged the *Vikram Protocol*,
and what survives of it is fragments of betrayal and exile that do not
reconstruct cleanly.
