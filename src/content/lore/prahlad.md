A gaunt, soft-spoken man.

Keeping an archive after the institutions have gone is not librarianship. It is
deciding, every day, what is worth the risk of holding — and being the person
who has to say no.

### The other Prahlad

The hero files carry the name too. In the entry on
[Vikram](/lore/vikram-malhotra), Prahlad is an early state-designed prototype who
*refused to displace coastal communities* and was decommissioned for it,
alongside [Asha](/lore/asha).

The archive has never been able to establish whether that is this man. Records
from either side of the Disclosures rarely survive in matching pairs, and a
common name is exactly the kind of thing that does not resolve. It is left
unmerged here on purpose: collapsing two people into one because it makes a
better story is the failure mode this whole archive exists to avoid.
