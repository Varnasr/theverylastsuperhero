Twenty years of research on climate adaptation, and the technology still
fascinates him — he adjusts the collar of his cooling suit and laughs about
Delhi to Kolkata in six hours.

He once worked on the Yamuna Action Plan. His work now is waste-to-energy: pilot
models to review for emissions integrity, new designs to implement, efficiency
reports. His enthusiasm for environmental solutions is lifelong and, to a
fourteen-year-old who has heard promises all her life, not obviously warranted.

Being a scientist in a state that has rewritten its own curriculum eventually
makes you a whistleblower whether or not you set out to be one.

He steps off at Serenity Junction to hand papers to a colleague. The train stops
for two minutes.

He and Meera fought to expose it. They failed. But they left codes behind.
