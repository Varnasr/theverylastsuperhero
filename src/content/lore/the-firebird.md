The Resistance had a symbol.

It was not on uniforms — there were none. It was not on government records — they
were erased. But it was everywhere: drawn in charcoal, etched in code, burned
into surveillance scrap.

A firebird with wings mid-rise, and one feather always missing.

The missing feather is the part people get wrong when they copy it, and getting
it wrong is how you know they were only copying. A bird caught mid-rise has not
risen yet. A feather short is not a flourish; it is an admission, kept in the
mark itself, that something was lost getting this far and will not be pretended
back into place.

It has no *official* form, and could not have one. A symbol that cannot be
standardised cannot be catalogued, and a symbol that cannot be catalogued cannot
be deleted. Hand-sketched, stitched, sprayed, remembered — every copy a little
different, every copy short one feather.
