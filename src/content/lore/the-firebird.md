Appearing in forgotten graffiti and old books, the Firebird signals the return of
belief, risk, and memory.

It has no official form. It is hand-sketched, stitched, sprayed, remembered. That
is the point: a symbol that cannot be standardised cannot be catalogued, and a
symbol that cannot be catalogued cannot be deleted.
