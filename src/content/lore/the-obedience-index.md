An algorithmic trust score, used to assign privileges, mobility rights and
employment status.

It is collected at compliance scoring terminals — kiosks that watch behaviour and
withhold services accordingly — and it follows a person the way a shadow does,
which is to say it is attached, it is not consulted, and it gets longer near the
end of the day.

The index does not publish its weights. This is presented as a security measure.

> Obedience Index: never assigned.
>
> — Citizen record, [Aasma](/lore/aasma), age 11

Aasma has no score because she has no record worth scoring: registered orphan,
guardian removed, never enrolled in anything that reports. The state cannot rate
her because it has already lost her. Her file closes *Status: possibly forgotten,
possibly protected*, and the archive has never been able to decide which of those
the index made true.

The name is the honest part. Nobody called it a merit index, or a citizenship
score. They called it what it measured.
