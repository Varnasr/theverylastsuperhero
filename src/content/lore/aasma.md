A clever, fast-talking girl living on the fringes of the surveillance grid.

Growing up outside the compliance index teaches a particular kind of literacy:
which corners the cameras have never learned, which queues move and which are
theatre, which adults will look at a child twice. She knows all of it, and she
is generous with it, which is rarer.

> Chandra sees everything. That's why they don't notice her.
