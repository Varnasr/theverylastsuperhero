Three of them, built the same and finishing differently.

The Rekhas rejected their control protocols and joined the Resistance. They are
recorded as the first synthetics to act with independent moral agency — which is
a careful way of saying they were the first to be given orders and decline them.

**REKHA-2** — decommissioned during an unauthorised flood reroute.
**REKHA-3** — missing. Last seen protecting a civilian data vault.

REKHA-1's record ends without a line, and the archive has never filled it in.

Their shrine is hidden in [Kishanganj](/lore/kishanganj). It is not marked, and
the people who tend it do not call it a shrine.

[Asha](/lore/asha) rerouted floodwater without clearance and was decommissioned
for it. REKHA-2 did the same thing and met the same end. The archive files them
apart — one a hero prototype, one a bio-robot — but the sentence is identical,
and so is the reason.
