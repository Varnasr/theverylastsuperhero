The wind-manipulating hero who promised clean air.

Sumati's mother kept a shrine to him.

That is the whole entry, and it is enough. Her parents' generation believed in
these people *completely* — not as celebrities but as intercession, something you
put a lamp in front of. A god of wind, named for the god of wind, promising the
one thing the air could no longer be trusted to provide.

The promise was not kept. The shrine is the part worth archiving: not what the
heroes did, but what people arranged their homes around while waiting.
