A waste-to-energy plant near Rajapur. Publicly clean. Privately toxic.

The ash comes every night, usually between midnight and four. By morning it is
on the plants, on the clothes hung out to dry, and on any food left uncovered.
The street sign on the way out of the station reads *Waste to Energy by Thakur
Green Developers*, which is the kind of name that is chosen rather than earned.

The plant is linked to disappearances. [Aasma](/lore/aasma) witnessed
unauthorised detentions at the Thakur facility, and her citizen record notes it
under *special note*, which is where the state files things it has decided not
to act on.

**Mr Thakur** runs it. He was an idealist once. The archive keeps that detail
because it is the useful part: the plant is not the work of someone who set out
to poison a district. It is the work of someone who kept agreeing to one more
adjustment of the declared tolerance.

The infiltration — led by Sunil, supported by Aasma and Satish — took the
internal documents out to external servers. That is how the disclosures got out
the second time, and this time nobody had a cape on.
