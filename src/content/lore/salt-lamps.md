Forged from crystalline Himalayan salt, the lamps store, relay, and sometimes
whisper living histories across a decentralised resistance network.

A lamp is not a hard drive. It holds testimony the way a room holds a
conversation — imperfectly, warmly, and only for those who know how to listen. A
lamp that has gone dark is not empty. It is waiting.

Salt Lamps went quiet across the network in 2073. One was lit again in 2075.
