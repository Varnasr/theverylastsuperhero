Technically, salt from the Khewra mines in Punjab.

Not the Himalayas. There are no salt pans in the actual mountains — never were,
never will be. But *Himalayan* sounds mystical and majestic, like a wellness
influencer wearing saffron and Wi-Fi, so that is what everyone calls it. The
lamps arrived with a costly PR team long before they arrived with a purpose.

What they actually are: precision-carved crystalline lattices of halite blended
with [hysterons](/lore/hysterons), designed by
[Myaknju](/lore/myaknju). Record, reflect, relay. They pick up history, not just
heat.

A lamp is not a hard drive. It holds testimony the way a room holds a
conversation — imperfectly, warmly, and only for those who know how to listen.
What comes back out is never quite what went in, and the Resistance decided long
ago that this was a feature. A record that must be re-spoken to survive is a
record with people attached to it.

They called it Story Salt. If you know how to listen, it tells you what happened
nearby.

Memory, mood, meaning, all glowing softly. But don't lick it. They tried that
once. The lamp got confused.

A lamp that has gone dark is not empty. It is waiting. Salt Lamps went quiet
across the network in 2073. One was lit again in 2075.
