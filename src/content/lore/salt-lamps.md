Forged from crystalline Himalayan salt and hysterons, the lamps store, relay,
and sometimes whisper the living oral histories of the Resistance across a
decentralised network.

A lamp is not a hard drive. It holds testimony the way a room holds a
conversation — imperfectly, warmly, and only for those who know how to listen.
What comes back out is never quite what went in, and the Resistance decided long
ago that this was a feature. A record that cannot be altered is a record nobody
tends. A record that must be re-spoken to survive is a record with people
attached to it.

Every lamp is therefore two technologies at once: a lattice that holds, and a
community that keeps re-lighting it. Lose either and the other is furniture.

A lamp that has gone dark is not empty. It is waiting.

Salt Lamps went quiet across the network in 2073. One was lit again in 2075.
