The environmental devastation that led to the downfall of cities and the rise of
the Resistance.

It was not a single day. It was the Bangladesh flood in 2055, the Disclosures in
2060, the curriculum rewrite in 2061, and the Yamuna breach in 2069 — a sequence
long enough that people kept expecting it to stop, and short enough that it never
did.
