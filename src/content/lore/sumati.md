The girl who finds her father's Salt Lamp and reawakens the forgotten archive.

Fourteen years old, in a Delhi that has been under water and under watch for most
of her life. She is not chosen, not enhanced, and not especially brave. She is
simply the one who is still listening when the lamp starts to speak.
