The scent of warm spices at the very end of the platform, under a sign reading
*Nandini's*.

Before that, she taught at the Sarvodaya Vidyalaya, until it collapsed. A school
that has fallen down does not stop having a teacher; it only stops having a
building, and she went on teaching in whatever room she found herself in.

To a fourteen-year-old who has missed her train and does not know what to do, she
offers the only sentence that helps: *we'll figure it out together.*
