*We need to get off here for a moment to hand over some important documents to
my colleague, Mr Joshi. Remember, the train will only stop for two minutes.*

He mentions, almost as an afterthought, that they are piloting a new WTE system.
Supposed to be cleaner, more efficient. A model for the whole zone, if it works.

Sumati does not pay much attention at the time. Afterwards, those words echo
differently.
