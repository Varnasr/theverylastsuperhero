Unlike the other birds, which scatter at anyone's approach, this one remains —
watching from a telephone pole, head tilted as if studying them.

*Shoo!* It merely shifts position and continues watching.

Later, cawing through a suffocating silence: ruffled feathers, beady black eyes,
cocking its head as if trying to communicate.

The explanation, when it comes, is from a tattered manuscript in the Treasure
Room, in an unfamiliar script:

> Sometimes, our dear ancestors return to us, in the form of ants or crows, to
> guide us through life, just as the moon, Chandra, guides the stars through the
> night sky.

A silent watcher who understands more than she should. Chandra sees everything.
That is precisely why nobody notices her.
