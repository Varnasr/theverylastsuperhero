The abandoned headquarters of the Delhi Climate Brigade, reclaimed and rebuilt by community effort. Where display cases once held superhero memorabilia, living walls of greenery grow. Where command centres once coordinated spectacular rescues, people now work on practical ones. A courtyard, a living laboratory, and schoolchildren being shown urban farming by a retired librarian.
