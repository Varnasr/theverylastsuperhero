---
term: "Waste-to-Energy (WTE) Plants"
order: 5
---

Touted as a green solution to India’s urban waste crisis, WTE plants promise to generate electricity from garbage. However, real-world research and media reports—such as a 2023 New York Times exposé on the Okhla plant in Delhi—show that these plants often emit toxic pollutants, including dioxins, and displace marginal communities. Rohan’s work on WTE systems in the novel represents technocratic ambition and moral compromise.
