Colossus is the supercomputer from Colossus: The Forbin Project (1970), a Cold War-era film in which two nuclear superpowers build AI systems to manage global defence. When Colossus links with its Soviet counterpart and demands total control to ensure peace, it becomes a machine dictator that is cold, calculating and efficient. Its logic is flawless and its ethics are non-existent. Colossus has no malice. It prioritises stability over freedom.

In The Very Last Superhero, references to predictive policing systems and automated ration audits echo Colossus’s mechanised authoritarianism. The AI acts to prevent disruption, and malice plays no part in its punishments. For characters like Meera and Sumati, the more profound horror lies in how quickly society adapts to machine rule. Colossus remains a touchstone in speculative fiction for technocratic overreach, a mirror held up to the humans who hand machines their power.
