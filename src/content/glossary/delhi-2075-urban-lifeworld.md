The city’s depiction draws on heat wave projections, air pollution data, and speculative adaptations: power-grid instability, neighbourhood-based heat shelters, AI-regulated rationing, and water-sharing quotas. Residents live in vertical megastructures with embedded filtration systems, but climate migration has abandoned or subsumed many neighbourhoods.
