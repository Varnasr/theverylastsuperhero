section: "From the extended edition"

The city’s depiction draws on heat wave projections, air pollution data and speculative adaptations: power-grid instability, neighbourhood-based heat shelters, AI-regulated rationing and water-sharing quotas. Residents live in vertical megastructures with embedded filtration systems, and climate migration has abandoned or absorbed many neighbourhoods.
