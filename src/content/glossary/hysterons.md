A fictional nanomaterial component used in the salt lamps designed by Resistance technologist Myaknju. In The Very Last Superhero, Hysterons are materials that react to and store input from vibrational energy— such as sound, heat, or electromagnetic signatures—within a stable crystalline structure like halite. By embedding Hysterons in salt lamp matrices, engineers created non-networked, analog memory systems that could “remember” environmental events and relay them when conditions were right.

The concept is inspired by real-world ideas in materials science (e.g., hysteresis in magnetic systems), where past inputs influence future states. In the story, they become the material infrastructure of resistance memory, allowing relics to record truth outside digital surveillance grids. Hysterons are not naturally occurring; they are engineered for resonance, recovery, and refusal to forget.
