Hysterons are a fictional nanomaterial component used in the salt lamps designed by Resistance technologist Myaknju. In The Very Last Superhero, Hysterons are materials that react to vibrational energy, such as sound, heat or electromagnetic signatures, and store it within a stable crystalline structure like halite. By embedding Hysterons in salt lamp matrices, engineers created non-networked analogue memory systems that could “remember” environmental events and relay them when conditions were right.

The concept is inspired by real-world ideas in materials science, such as hysteresis in magnetic systems, where past inputs influence future states. In the story Hysterons become the material infrastructure of resistance memory, which allows relics to record truth outside digital surveillance grids. Hysterons do not occur naturally. They are engineered for resonance, recovery and refusal to forget.
