term: "Virtual layer flooring"
section: "Core technologies and systems"

---
The virtual layer of flooring is a modular sensory surface installed on high-speed transit systems like the Hyper-Express. It provides interactive feedback underfoot. It was developed initially as an accessibility feature, and it uses pressure-sensitive smart polymers embedded with micro projectors to emit visual cues such as light paths, alerts or emergency signals, based on movement, foot pressure or programmed schedules. In commercial use by 2068, this flooring will be standard in state-run maglev and intercity transport.

Passengers walking across it leave transient trails of soft-coloured light, often coded for visibility or status (for example, red trails for security personnel and blue for standard passengers). The system syncs with the train AI to update directions during boarding, redirect foot traffic or identify anomalies in gait that might indicate distress. Rohan Kapoor walks across it shortly after being separated from Sumati and leaves a “blue blur of soft light,” a visual trace that symbolises both presence and absence.

The flooring improves safety and navigation. Surveillance systems also use it to map passenger movement, feed behavioural data into predictive models and flag deviations from assigned zones. Like many technologies in The Very Last Superhero, the virtual floor is convenient and is also a tool of control that quietly shapes how people move through a monitored space.
