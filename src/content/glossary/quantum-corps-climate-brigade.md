---
term: "Quantum Corps / Climate Brigade"
order: 3
---

These were India’s state-supported climate-response superhero coalitions, founded during the 2030s climate emergency years. The Quantum Corps specialised in planetary interventions—redirecting hurricanes, manipulating rainfall patterns, and geoengineering. The Climate Brigade focused on more localised interventions: heat shields, wildfire barriers, and disaster evacuations. Idolised at first, they were later condemned when it emerged that many had collaborated with corporations profiting from environmental degradation.
