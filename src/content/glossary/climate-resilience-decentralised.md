One of the novel’s underlying arguments is that climate resilience comes from systems that ordinary people keep running, and heroism plays no part in it. Nandini Maa’s tea stall, the local post office and the kindness of strangers are the engines of resilience. In Sumati’s story, community counts for more than spectacle.
