The story contains echoes of Bollywood shrine culture, superhero worship and the climate disaster documentaries that once filled 24-hour news cycles. The novel’s defaced superhero statues and holographic memorials are physical remnants of broken trust. These moments reflect how societies mythologise and discard their saviours, whether heroes, politicians or technologies.
