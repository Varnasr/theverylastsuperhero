In a world overwhelmed by digital surveillance and electromagnetic overload, feature phones—simple, unconnected devices—have made a quiet comeback. Many children in Sumati’s world are issued these for emergency use only. Their presence signifies a broader shift: back to minimalism, back to analogue, and a renewed focus on privacy, durability, and simplicity.
