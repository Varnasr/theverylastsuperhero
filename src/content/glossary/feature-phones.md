In a world overwhelmed by digital surveillance and electromagnetic overload, feature phones, which are simple and unconnected devices, have made a quiet comeback. Many children in Sumati’s world are issued one for emergency use only. Their return signals a broader shift back to minimalism and analogue tools, with a renewed focus on privacy, durability and simplicity.
