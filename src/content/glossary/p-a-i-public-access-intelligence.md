---
term: "P.A.I. (Public Access Intelligence)"
section: "AI Systems and Surveillance Technologies"
order: 5
---

P.A.I. is the dominant state-managed artificial intelligence system in the world of The Very Last Superhero. Presented publicly as a decentralised platform for climate monitoring and civic efficiency, its function is far more insidious. Built by a joint syndicate of state ministries and corporate interests, P.A.I. tracks citizen behaviour, predicts dissent, and reroutes enforcement drones to suppress uprisings. It is modular and opaque, adapting quickly without transparency or oversight. Its surveillance net spans biometric terminals, comms devices, school attendance logs, and more. In the novel, P.A.I. represents the logical end-point of predictive policing and civic scoring—a system where dissent is forecast and neutralised before it begins. Its logic is cold, utilitarian, and unaccountable. The system’s name is a pun: it implies public access, but in reality, it governs the public while being entirely inaccessible to them. P.A.I.’s presence haunts the early chapters of Sumati’s journey—it is why she cannot call for help, her identity becomes fragmented, and her father’s disappearance is treated as a system glitch, not a crisis. P.A.I. is what happens when machine logic becomes the moral compass of a society.
