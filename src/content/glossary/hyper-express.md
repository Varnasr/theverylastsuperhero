The Hyper-Express is India’s flagship maglev rail project, launched post-2060 to replace long-haul domestic flights and reduce aviation-related carbon emissions. It can cross the subcontinent in hours and is sleek and energy-efficient, symbolising high modernist optimism. But for Sumati, it also becomes a site of dislocation and loss—a reminder that infrastructure cannot substitute for care.
