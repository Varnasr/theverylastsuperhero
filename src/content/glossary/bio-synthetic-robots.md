term: "Bio-synthetic robots"
section: "Surveillance tools and public infrastructure"

---
Bio-synthetic robots, also known as biosynths, are hybrid entities made of organic tissue and synthetic frameworks. They were developed through a combination of advanced prosthetics, gene-editing and machine intelligence. They were once hailed as the future of climate-response personnel, because they could withstand extreme environments, could heal and were said to be more “humane” than traditional AI. During the superhero era (2035–2060), many public figures within the Quantum Corps and the Delhi Climate Brigade were later revealed to be early-generation biosynths, modified beyond recognition in corporate biolabs.

After the 2060 Disclosures the biosynths were officially decommissioned, and most records of their development were sealed or erased. In The Very Last Superhero they appear only in rumours and half-buried files: fragmented video logs, degraded medical scans and whispered confessions from failed trial participants. Unlike purely robotic systems, biosynths complicate the line between human and machine. Their autonomy is partial, their consent is unclear, and their bodies were designed for function over selfhood.

Resistance archives debate whether biosynths should be mourned or dismantled. For Sumati they remain part of the eerie backdrop of a world that once trusted altered bodies to solve unaltered problems. Their legacy is what they were forced to become.
