---
term: "Audible Enclaves"
section: "Surveillance Tools and Public Infrastructure"
order: 15
---
A precision sound technology that allows a message to be heard by a specific person in a shared space, without headphones, without volume, and without others hearing it. Developed initially using intersecting ultrasonic beams, it creates a tight “listening bubble” that follows the intended recipient. In the Resistance’s adaptation, enclave acoustics were embedded into salt lamp infrastructure to carry memory, warnings, or archived sound across collapsing systems.

The tech enables moments where a lamp “speaks” not to the room, but to a single person. Often mistaken for intuition or hallucination, the enclave signal is actual, but only when it’s time. This is based on real tech, initially developed by researchers at Pennsylvania State University in 2024. The system uses intersecting ultrasonic beams to create a private “listening bubble” around an individual. The effect is real-time, mobile, and undetectable to others in the same environment.
