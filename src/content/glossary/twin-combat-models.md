term: "Twin Combat Models"
section: "Surveillance tools and public infrastructure"

Twin Combat Models were a paired class of biosynthetic humanoid units developed during the late superhero period for coordinated climate response and urban conflict resolution. They were deployed in mirrored pairs, biometrically linked to respond in perfect synchrony, and were often trained to show complementary tactical behaviours, for example one defensive and one offensive. Official documentation claimed the pairing increased field efficiency and mutual regulation.

Unofficial sources suggest the twins were designed to prevent defection or autonomous decision-making, since one unit’s failure would trigger the deactivation of the other. In The Very Last Superhero their presence is implied through fragmented footage and testimonies, particularly in references to a scorched village where two identical figures moved with inhuman precision. They are rumoured to have been deployed in both environmental crises and protest suppression, and the Twin Models blurred the line between aid and control.

Many resistance historians classify them as proto-weapons disguised as humanitarian units. By 2075 few remain active, and some are said to have “gone rogue,” their twin-link corrupted or lost. They remain a symbol of ethical ambiguity: designed for unity, programmed for obedience and remembered with unease. Nobody ever made their names public, and their faces were always the same.
