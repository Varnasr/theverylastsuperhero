---
term: "Twin Combat Models"
section: "Surveillance Tools and Public Infrastructure"
order: 26
---

Refers to a paired class of biosynthetic humanoid units developed during the late superhero period, designed for coordinated climate-response and urban conflict resolution. The Twin Combat Models were deployed in mirrored pairs—biometrically linked to respond in perfect synchrony, often trained to exhibit complementary tactical behaviours (e.g., one defensive, one offensive). Official documentation claimed the pairing was to increase field efficiency and mutual regulation. Unofficial sources suggest the twins were designed to prevent defection or autonomous decision-making; one unit’s failure would trigger deactivation of the other. In The Very Last Superhero, their presence is implied through fragmented footage and testimonies, particularly in references to a scorched village where two identical figures moved with inhuman precision. Rumoured to have been deployed in both environmental crises and protest suppression, Twin Models blurred the line between aid and control. Many resistance historians classify them as proto-weapons disguised as humanitarian units. By 2075, few remain active; some are said to have “gone rogue,” their twinlink corrupted or lost. They remain a symbol of ethical ambiguity: designed for unity, programmed for obedience, and remembered with unease. Their names were never public. Their faces were always the same.
