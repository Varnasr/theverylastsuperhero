These mathematical concepts come from linear algebra but have real-world applications in systems analysis. In power grids, eigenvalues help determine whether minor disturbances will dissipate or cascade into blackouts. In structural engineering, they help assess stability. Sumati’s mention of eigenvalues during a math class hints at how her mind is wired—towards structure, pattern, and systems thinking. It's also a metaphor: What remains stable when a society’s internal matrix changes?
