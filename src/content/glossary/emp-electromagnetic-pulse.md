An EMP is an intense burst of electromagnetic radiation that can turn off electronic circuits, communications infrastructure and unshielded digital memory systems. EMPs can occur naturally, from solar flares, or be generated artificially, often by weaponised devices. In The Very Last Superhero, EMPs are referenced as tactical tools and as historical events, including during the dismantling of early resistance networks and the collapse of superhero command nodes in the Northern corridors.

The 2060 Disclosures revealed that state forces used EMPs to suppress rogue AI activity and destroy evidence, while unofficial archives suggest that internal saboteurs within the Brigade deployed some of them. Modern infrastructure claims to be EMP-resistant, but anecdotal failures persist. Sumati’s satcomm is hardened and is still vulnerable under specific field intensities. For resistance cells, the threat of EMP has led to a resurgence in analogue tools, offline archives and physically encoded memory systems such as salt crystals, paper and spoken record.

The cultural fear of EMPs is technical, and it is also symbolic of sudden, engineered memory loss. In a society built on fragile connections, the idea that everything could vanish in a pulse is both a tactical possibility and a metaphor for forgetting.
