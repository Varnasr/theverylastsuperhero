---
term: "Cooling Suit"
order: 2
---

The urban middle class will wear cooling suits every day in 2075. They modulate body temperature by using thermoelectric cooling, nanomaterials, and lightweight graphene composites. First deployed by military forces and disaster responders, cooling suits became standard as daily highs in cities crossed 50°C. Yet they remain unaffordable for many, and their presence in the novel underscores class divides in climate adaptation.
