In the novel’s timeline, quantum computing has become central to environmental modelling, cryptographic security, and interstate negotiations over water and emissions. These systems can solve complex problems like protein folding and carbon trading equilibria, but their opacity and susceptibility to manipulation have also introduced new ethical dilemmas. As Sumati learns, high computation does not equal high wisdom.
