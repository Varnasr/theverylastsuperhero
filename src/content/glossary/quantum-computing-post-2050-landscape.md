In the novel’s timeline, quantum computing has become central to environmental modelling, cryptographic security and interstate negotiations over water and emissions. These systems can solve complex problems such as protein folding and carbon trading equilibria. Their opacity and their susceptibility to manipulation have also introduced new ethical dilemmas. Sumati learns that high computation brings no guarantee of high wisdom.
