---
term: "Eco-Park Kolkata"
section: "From the Extended Edition"
order: 108
---
A real urban green space in New Town, Kolkata, is reimagined in the novel as a futuristic hub for environmental education, renewable energy tech, and climate adaptation research. It includes biodomes, holographic climate simulations, aquaponics labs, and AR-enhanced journeys through Earth's history. For Sumati, it represents one of the few glimpses of a positive ecological future—one not dependent on caped heroes.
