Eco-Park is a real urban green space in New Town, Kolkata. The novel reimagines it as a futuristic hub for environmental education, renewable energy technology and climate adaptation research, with biodomes, holographic climate simulations, aquaponics labs and AR-enhanced tours of Earth’s history. For Sumati it is one of the few glimpses of a positive ecological future, one that does not depend on caped heroes.
