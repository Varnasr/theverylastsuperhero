Pandemonium architecture is a fundamental concept in early artificial intelligence, proposed by Oliver Selfridge in 1959. It imagines the mind as a set of “demons” or agents, each with a specific role such as feature detection or decision-making, all shouting to be heard. By 2075 this metaphor was repurposed to describe India’s sprawling, distributed AI systems: overlapping municipal algorithms, transit AIs and surveillance bots, all competing and interacting in unpredictable ways.
