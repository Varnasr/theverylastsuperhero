---
term: "Pandemonium Architecture (AI History)"
section: "From the Extended Edition"
order: 106
---
A fundamental concept in early artificial intelligence, it was proposed by Oliver Selfridge in 1959. It imagines the mind as a set of “demons” or agents, each with specific roles (e.g., feature detection, decision-making), all shouting to be heard. By 2075, this metaphor was repurposed to describe India’s sprawling, distributed AI systems: overlapping municipal algorithms, transit AIs, and surveillance bots, all competing and interacting in unpredictable ways.
