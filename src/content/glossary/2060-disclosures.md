term: "2060 Disclosures"
order: 4
---

This term refers to the global investigative exposés and tribunal trials that revealed the failings and corruption of the superhero era. Key revelations included that weather manipulation exacerbated water insecurity, fossil fuel corporations funded superhero salaries, and “climate solutions” were often distractions from needed structural change. The fallout was immediate: public trust collapsed, statues were torn down, and superhero programmes were dismantled.
