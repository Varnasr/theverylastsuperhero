Simple, unconnected, durable. The kind of device that came back into use once
everything else had become a surveillance surface.

Many children in Sumati's world are issued one for emergency use only. The
return of the feature phone is usually described as nostalgia. It is not. It is
a considered retreat: back to minimalism, back to analogue, back to a handset
that cannot be asked where it has been.

Her father gave her hers. That is the entire specification that matters.
