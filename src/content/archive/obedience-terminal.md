A public kiosk. It reads a biometric ID, consults the
[Obedience Index](/lore/the-obedience-index), and decides what you may have
today.

This one was recovered intact and unvandalised from a district where almost
nothing else survived. The catalogue notes that as a finding rather than a
convenience. Statues of heroes were pulled down within weeks of the Disclosures.
The terminals stayed up, because the terminals were still issuing rations.

You do not smash the thing that feeds you, even when it is also the thing
counting you.
