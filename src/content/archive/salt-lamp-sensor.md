Recovered from a plank-walled room with straw on the floor, where it had been
sitting in the middle of the space giving off a low red glow and no heat worth
speaking of.

The catalogue cannot decide what it is. Hybrid memory-storage, or a sensor, or
both, or a lamp that has been in one room long enough to have opinions about it.
The distinction matters less than the cataloguers would like: a lamp records
whether or not anyone intended it to.

It was not hidden. It was in the middle of the floor, lit, in a room someone was
living in.
