Rumoured. That word is doing all the work in this entry.

Bio-drones, allegedly, built for covert surveillance and shaped like the one bird
nobody in an Indian city ever looks at twice. No confirmed specimen has been
catalogued. Either they never existed, or they are indistinguishable from crows,
which was the entire point of building them.

[Chandra](/lore/chandra) is the reason the file stays open. She is larger than
most, has a white patch on one wing, turns up at relic sites, and kept a girl's
school ID safe for a year. The archive has never established whether that is
training, engineering, or a crow.

The manuscript in the Treasure Room offers a third reading: that ancestors return
as ants or crows, to guide us. The catalogue records this alongside the
surveillance hypothesis without ranking them.
