About the book

Heroism, after the heroes

The Very Last Superhero is set in a near-future India where climate collapse, authoritarian control, and digital decay have stripped the world of its myths — and its memory. Fourteen-year-old Sumati inherits a Salt Lamp from her missing father: not a relic, but a device that stores oral histories.

In a Delhi buried beneath surveillance and floodwater, she sets out to reboot the Resistance archive and uncover what came before the Collapse. In this world, heroism is not superpowers or spectacle. It is memory, care, and the courage to hold a community together when everything else has fallen apart.

Salt Lamps

Crystalline lamps of Khewra salt blended with hysterons, carved into precision lattices that record, reflect and relay. Not Himalayan — that part was always a marketing scam.

The Firebird

A firebird with wings mid-rise and one feather always missing. Not on uniforms — there were none. Not on records — they were erased. Everywhere else.

Waste-to-Energy Systems

The pilot plants Rohan Kapoor reviews — revivals of the old Belgian system that worked at Gazipur in the 2020s, promising to revolutionise Delhi's energy grid if the emissions controls hold.
