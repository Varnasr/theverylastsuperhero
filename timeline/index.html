Chronology

Collapse & Resistance

12 recorded events. Entries marked redacted were recovered only in fragments.

  1. 2040s–2050s

    The Heroic Period

    For two decades, state- and corporate-backed heroes publicly tackled crime, pollution and dissent. Surveillance, welcomed at first, became ordinary.

    The Age of Heroes · Delhi Climate Brigade · Quantum Corps · P.A.I.

  2. 2055

    The Great Storms

    Captain Indra's weather-control technologies saved countless lives. Mass climate migration intensified, and coastal evacuation policies produced heavily militarised relief zones.

    Captain Indra · The Collapse · Cooling Suits

  3. 2060

    The Disclosures

    A leak showed Captain Indra had taken millions from the corporations he claimed to fight. The tribunals followed, the statues came down, and the age of heroes ended.

    The Age of Heroes · Captain Indra · Delhi Climate Brigade · Quantum Corps

  4. 2061

    Collapse of Trust

    The AI-generated National Curriculum was introduced. History was revised; unauthorised records of the Resistance were deleted.

    P.A.I. · The Collapse

  5. 2061

    Spray-painted near Okhla

    Every empire that forgets its own data will be remembered by its wreckage. The Resistance slogan appears the same year the curriculum is rewritten.

    The Resistance · P.A.I.

  6. 2065

    P.A.I. Deployed Across the Metros

    Public AI Surveillance reached full civic deployment in every major metro, automating compliance scoring and behavioural prediction at national scale.

    P.A.I.

  7. 2065

    Resistance Formation

    Clusters of oral historians, students, archivists, and rogue librarians began training others in salt lamp recovery.

    The Resistance · Salt Lamps

  8. 2069

    The Yamuna Breach

    Heavy rainfall combined with blocked drainage led to the collapse of four embankments. Thousands were displaced. Delhi drowned.

    The Collapse

  9. 2070

    The Whisper Sabotage

    p.ai sabotage operations began in earnest against the surveillance network. Lockdowns followed across the監 zones.

    p.ai · P.A.I.

  10. 2072

    Rohan Kapoor Disappears

    During a raid on the Archive Vault, Rohan vanishes. His last known location was near the submerged Satcomm tower.

    Rohan Kapoor · The Treasure Room

  11. 2073

    The Archive Goes Dark

    The Salt Lamp archive deactivates across the network. For two years, no lamp answers.

    Salt Lamps · The Treasure Room

  12. 2075

    Sumati Activates the Lamp

    A young girl uncovers an old Salt Lamp. Stories whisper back. The Firebird returns. The Archive begins to pulse again.

    Sumati · Salt Lamps · The Firebird
